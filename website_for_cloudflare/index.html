Master Formula Compendium & Physical Constants (Ch 1 – 8)

Fundamental Physical Constants:
Avogadro's Number: \(N_A = 6.022 \times 10^{23} \text{ atoms/mol}\) • Boltzmann's Constant: \(k = 1.38 \times 10^{-23} \text{ J/atom}\cdot\text{K} = 8.62 \times 10^{-5} \text{ eV/atom}\cdot\text{K}\)
Universal Gas Constant: \(R = 8.314 \text{ J/mol}\cdot\text{K}\) • Elementary Charge: \(e = 1.602 \times 10^{-19} \text{ C}\) • 1 eV = \(1.602 \times 10^{-19} \text{ J}\)
Domain / Law Governing Mathematical Equation Key Variables & Units
Theoretical Crystal Density $$\rho = \frac{n \cdot A}{V_c \cdot N_A}$$ \(n\): atoms/cell; \(A\): g/mol; \(V_c\): cell volume (\(\text{cm}^3\)); \(\rho\): \(\text{g/cm}^3\)
Lattice Constants & APF BCC: \(a = \frac{4R}{\sqrt{3}}\), \(\text{APF} = 0.68\), \(n=2\)
FCC: \(a = 2\sqrt{2}R\), \(\text{APF} = 0.74\), \(n=4\) 		\(R\): atomic radius; \(a\): lattice parameter; \(\text{APF} = \frac{n(4/3\pi R^3)}{V_c}\)
Cubic Interplanar Spacing $$d_{hkl} = \frac{a}{\sqrt{h^2 + k^2 + l^2}}$$ \((hkl)\): Miller indices; \(a\): unit cell lattice parameter
Bragg's Law (XRD) $$n\lambda = 2 d_{hkl} \sin\theta$$ \(\lambda\): X-ray wavelength; \(\theta\): Bragg diffraction angle; \(n\): order (usually 1)
Equilibrium Vacancies $$N_v = N \exp\left(-\frac{Q_v}{kT}\right)$$ \(N\): total atomic sites; \(Q_v\): vacancy activation energy (eV or J/mol); \(T\): Kelvin
ASTM Grain Size Number $$N = 2^{n - 1}$$ \(N\): grains per square inch at 100\(\times\); \(n\): ASTM grain size number
Fick's First Law (Steady-State) $$J = -D \frac{dC}{dx} = \frac{M}{A \cdot t}$$ \(J\): flux (\(\text{kg/m}^2\cdot\text{s}\)); \(D\): diffusivity (\(\text{m}^2/\text{s}\)); \(dC/dx\): gradient
Fick's Second Law (Semi-Infinite) $$\frac{C_x - C_0}{C_s - C_0} = 1 - \text{erf}\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$ \(C_s\): surface conc; \(C_0\): initial base conc; \(C_x\): conc at depth \(x\) after time \(t\)
Arrhenius Temperature Law $$D = D_0 \exp\left(-\frac{Q_d}{RT}\right)$$ \(D_0\): pre-exponential (\(\text{m}^2/\text{s}\)); \(Q_d\): activation energy (\(\text{J/mol}\)); \(R=8.314\)
Hooke's Law & Shear Modulus $$\sigma = E\varepsilon, \quad \tau = G\gamma, \quad G = \frac{E}{2(1 + \nu)}$$ \(E\): Young's modulus; \(G\): Shear modulus; \(\nu\): Poisson's ratio (\(\sim 0.3\))
True Stress & True Strain $$\sigma_T = \sigma(1+\varepsilon), \quad \varepsilon_T = \ln(1+\varepsilon)$$ Valid up to necking; \(\sigma, \varepsilon\): engineering stress/strain
Hollomon Power Law $$\sigma_T = K \varepsilon_T^n$$ \(K\): strength coefficient; \(n\): strain hardening exponent; Necking: \(\varepsilon_T = n\)
Modulus of Resilience $$U_r = \int_0^{\varepsilon_y} \sigma d\varepsilon \approx \frac{\sigma_y^2}{2E}$$ \(U_r\): elastic energy stored per unit volume (\(\text{J/m}^3\) or \(\text{Pa}\))
Schmid's Law (Single Crystals) $$\tau_R = \sigma \cos\phi \cos\lambda \le \tau_{\text{CRSS}}$$ \(\phi\): angle to slip plane normal; \(\lambda\): angle to slip direction; \(m = \cos\phi\cos\lambda\)
Hall-Petch Strengthening $$\sigma_y = \sigma_0 + k_y d^{-1/2}$$ \(\sigma_0\): friction stress; \(k_y\): locking coefficient (\(\text{MPa}\cdot\text{mm}^{1/2}\)); \(d\): grain size
Cold Work & Dislocation Density $$\%\text{CW} = \frac{A_0 - A_d}{A_0} \times 100\%, \quad \tau = \tau_0 + \alpha G b \sqrt{\rho}$$ \(\rho\): dislocation density (\(\text{m}^{-2}\)); \(b\): Burgers vector; \(A_0, A_d\): cross-sections
Orowan Looping (Precipitation) $$\Delta\tau = \frac{G b}{L - 2r} \approx \frac{G b}{L}$$ \(L\): inter-particle spacing; \(r\): particle radius; \(G\): matrix shear modulus
Stress Concentration (Crack Tip) $$K_t = \frac{\sigma_m}{\sigma_0} = 2 \sqrt{\frac{a}{\rho_t}}$$ \(a\): crack length; \(\rho_t\): crack tip radius of curvature; \(\sigma_0\): nominal stress
Griffith Critical Fracture Stress $$\sigma_c = \sqrt{\frac{2E\gamma_s}{\pi a}}$$ \(\gamma_s\): specific surface energy (\(\text{J/m}^2\)); \(E\): Young's modulus; \(a\): crack size
Mode I Fracture Toughness $$K_I = Y \sigma \sqrt{\pi a} \le K_{Ic}$$ \(K_{Ic}\): plane strain fracture toughness (\(\text{MPa}\cdot\sqrt{\text{m}}\)); \(Y\): geometric factor
Fatigue Stress Parameters $$\sigma_m = \frac{\sigma_{\max}+\sigma_{\min}}{2}, \quad \sigma_a = \frac{\sigma_{\max}-\sigma_{\min}}{2}, \quad R = \frac{\sigma_{\min}}{\sigma_{\max}}$$ \(\sigma_m\): mean stress; \(\sigma_a\): amplitude; \(R\): stress ratio
Steady-State Creep Rate $$\dot{\varepsilon}_s = K_2 \sigma^n \exp\left(-\frac{Q_c}{RT}\right)$$ \(\dot{\varepsilon}_s\): steady creep rate (\(\text{s}^{-1}\)); \(n\): stress exponent; \(Q_c\): creep activation energy
Larson-Miller Parameter $$P = T \left(20 + \log_{10} t_r\right)$$ \(T\): absolute temperature in Kelvin; \(t_r\): rupture life in hours
