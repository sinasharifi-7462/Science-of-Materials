Master Formula Compendium & Physical Constants (Chapters 1 – 8)

Fundamental Physical Constants:
Avogadro's Number: \(N_A = 6.022 \times 10^{23} \text{ atoms/mol}\) • Boltzmann's Constant: \(k_B = 1.381 \times 10^{-23} \text{ J/K} = 8.617 \times 10^{-5} \text{ eV/K}\)
Universal Gas Constant: \(R = 8.314 \text{ J/mol}\cdot\text{K}\) • Electron Charge: \(e = 1.602 \times 10^{-19} \text{ C}\) • \(1 \text{ eV} = 1.602 \times 10^{-19} \text{ J}\)
Chapter / Topic Governing Equation & Relation Parameter Definitions
Ch 1: Ashby Indices $$M_1 = \frac{\sigma_y}{\rho}, \quad M_2 = \frac{E^{1/2}}{\rho}, \quad M_3 = \frac{\sigma_y^{2/3}}{\rho}$$ Lightweight tension tie-rod (\(M_1\)), stiff beam (\(M_2\)), strong beam (\(M_3\))
Ch 1: Composite Moduli $$E_{cl} = V_f E_f + V_m E_m, \quad \frac{1}{E_{ct}} = \frac{V_f}{E_f} + \frac{V_m}{E_m}$$ Voigt isostrain (longitudinal) and Reuss isostress (transverse) Rule of Mixtures
Ch 2: Potential Energy Well $$E_N(r) = -\frac{A}{r^m} + \frac{B}{r^n}, \quad F_N(r) = \frac{dE_N}{dr}$$ Attractive vs repulsive potential; equilibrium spacing \(r_0\) at \(F_N(r_0) = 0\)
Ch 3: Theoretical Density $$\rho = \frac{n \cdot A}{V_c \cdot N_A}$$ \(n\): atoms/cell; \(A\): atomic weight (g/mol); \(V_c\): cell volume
Ch 3: Crystal Geometry BCC: \(a = \frac{4R}{\sqrt{3}}\), \(\text{APF} = 0.68\)
FCC: \(a = 2\sqrt{2}R\), \(\text{APF} = 0.74\)		 \(R\): atomic radius; \(a\): cubic lattice parameter
Ch 3: Bragg's Law (XRD) $$n\lambda = 2 d_{hkl} \sin\theta, \quad d_{hkl} = \frac{a}{\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}}$$ \(\lambda\): X-ray wavelength; \(d_{hkl}\): interplanar spacing; \(\theta\): Bragg angle
Ch 4: Equilibrium Vacancies $$N_v = N \exp\left(-\frac{Q_v}{k_B T}\right)$$ \(N\): total atomic sites; \(Q_v\): vacancy formation energy
Ch 4: ASTM Grain Size $$N = 2^{n - 1}$$ \(N\): grains per square inch at 100\(\times\) magnification; \(n\): ASTM number
Ch 5: Fick's 1st Law $$J = -D \frac{dC}{dx}$$ \(J\): diffusion flux (\(\text{kg/m}^2\cdot\text{s}\)); \(D\): diffusion coefficient
Ch 5: Fick's 2nd Law $$\frac{C_x - C_0}{C_s - C_0} = 1 - \text{erf}\left(\frac{x}{2\sqrt{Dt}}\right)$$ Semi-infinite solid constant surface concentration carburization/decarburization
Ch 5: Arrhenius Diffusivity $$D = D_0 \exp\left(-\frac{Q_d}{RT}\right)$$ \(D_0\): pre-exponential; \(Q_d\): activation energy for diffusion
Ch 6: Hooke's Law & Moduli $$\sigma = E\varepsilon, \quad \tau = G\gamma, \quad G = \frac{E}{2(1 + \nu)}$$ \(E\): Young's modulus; \(G\): shear modulus; \(\nu\): Poisson's ratio
Ch 6: Modulus of Resilience $$U_r = \frac{\sigma_y^2}{2E}$$ Elastic strain energy storage capacity per unit volume up to yielding
Ch 6: Schmid's Law $$\tau_R = \sigma \cos\phi \cos\lambda \le \tau_{\text{CRSS}}$$ \(\phi\): normal to slip plane; \(\lambda\): slip direction angle
Ch 7: Hall-Petch Law $$\sigma_y = \sigma_0 + k_y d^{-1/2}$$ \(\sigma_0\): friction stress; \(k_y\): unpinning coefficient; \(d\): average grain diameter
Ch 7: Cold Work & Orowan $$\%\text{CW} = \frac{A_0 - A_d}{A_0} \times 100\%, \quad \Delta\tau = \frac{G b}{L}$$ Dislocation density multiplication & precipitate bowing resistance
Ch 8: Fracture Toughness $$K = Y \sigma \sqrt{\pi a} \le K_{Ic}$$ \(K_{Ic}\): plane strain fracture toughness; \(Y\): dimensionless crack geometry factor
Ch 8: Larson-Miller Creep $$P = T \left(20 + \log_{10} t_r\right)$$ \(T\): temperature in Kelvin; \(t_r\): creep rupture time in hours
